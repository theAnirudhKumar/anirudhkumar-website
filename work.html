Approach

How a new account gets built out

Diagnose

Read the account's actual usage before the first call. Most onboarding plans are written from the sales deck, not the product data.

Build

Onboarding flow, adoption tracking, and a health score, in that order. No health score is trustworthy without the tracking underneath it.

Instrument

Wire the health score into a system that surfaces risk automatically. If it lives in a spreadsheet someone has to remember to open, it doesn't work.

Iterate

Revisit the playbook every quarter against what actually happened, not what the plan predicted. The plan is wrong until proven otherwise.
