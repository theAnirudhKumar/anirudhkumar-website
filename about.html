About

The playbook doesn't exist yet. That's the job.

I'm Anirudh, a Senior Customer Success Manager working with early-stage SaaS companies where processes don't exist yet, and I build them. I've spent my career in the gap between "we should have a system for this" and actually having one.

The goal is always the same: make customer success predictable, not accidental. That means building onboarding flows, adoption tracking, health scoring, and expansion playbooks before anyone asks for them, not after churn makes the case.

Outside of the day job, I write and build in public. Hustlyst is a newsletter for people who want to use AI and automation without a technical background. CS Pulse Community is a small workshop series for CS practitioners who want the honest version of the job, not the highlight reel.
